In Auto Mode, users don’t need manual operation—files will sync automatically.

Click the cloud icon (sidebar <i class="fas fa-cloud"></i> on PC, or <i class="fas fa-cloud"></i> icon in the bottom hamburger menu <i class="fa fa-bars"></i> on mobile) to open the sync control view.<img src="/img/sync-vault-auto-mode.webp" alt="sync vault auto mode view" width="75%">

### Stop and Restart Auto Sync

Click the `Stop/Start syncing` button to stop or restart auto sync.

### View Sync Report

Click the `View sync report` button to check the sync report.
