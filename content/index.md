Sync Vault is a professional-grade Obsidian plugin that redefines synchronization. It combines the massive storage of public clouds with the speed of local notes, now **fully empowered by VFS and AI**.

## Who Needs Sync Vault?

- **Space Conscious**: If your vault is full of large PDFs and videos, and your hard drive is screaming.
- **Video Note-Takers**: If you want to take structured notes on cloud videos without downloading them.
- **AI Enthusiasts**: If you want **AI to read your TBs of cloud files** instantly via MCP.
- **Efficiency Seekers**: If you already have VIP accounts with **Baidu, Aliyun, or Quark**.
- **Collaborators**: If you need real-time P2P Live Sync for team collaboration.

## The 1+N Architecture: Why It’s Different

Sync Vault isn't just a "sync" tool; it's a **Cloud-Integrated File System**.
- **"1" (The Cloud)**: Your central archive (Baidu, Aliyun, etc.) holds everything.
- **"N" (Your Devices)**: Only the files you are currently working on stay local.
- **VFS (Virtual File System)**: Files appear in your vault as tiny stubs (`.osvc`). They occupy **zero space** until you click to "hydrate" them.

## Key Features

### 🔄 More Than Syncing
1. **VFS Virtualization**: View TBs of cloud data in your local sidebar with zero lag.
2. **P2P Live Sync**: Real-time cursor and edit sharing between devices via WebRTC.
3. **Smart Archiving**: Automatically offload old files back to the cloud to save local space.

### 🎥 Professional Video Workflow
1. **Cloud Streaming**: High-speed streaming for Baidu/Aliyun/Quark (up to 4K).
2. **Interactive Timestamps**: Right-click to insert a timestamp; click it to **jump back** to that exact second in the video.
3. **Side-by-Side Mode**: Dedicated player view for immersive study or film analysis.

### 🧠 AI Integration (MCP)
- **Chat with Cloud**: Connect Claude or Cursor to your vault. Ask questions about thousands of cloud PDFs without ever downloading them.
- **Semantic Search**: Find files based on meaning, not just filenames.
