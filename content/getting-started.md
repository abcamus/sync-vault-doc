> [!important] Note:
> 
> 1. Please disable other synchronization plugins when using Sync Vault, including the official Obsidian Sync.
> 2. Do not place your vault in synchronization directories such as iCloud Sync Folder or Baidu Sync Space while using Sync Vault; otherwise, unpredictable errors may occur.
> 3. It is recommended to disable proxies during cloud disk synchronization, as proxies may cause abnormal cloud disk access.

## Quick Setup

### Authorize
1. Navigate to the plugin settings page.
2. Click the "Get Started" tab.
3. Click the "Start Authorization" button and follow the on-screen instructions to complete cloud disk authorization.
> [!note] The welcome page
> After successful authorization, you will be redirected to a [welcome page](https://kqiu.top/welcome-to-sync-vault/). Follow the steps described there or as below 👇🏻
> 
### Open dashboard
Find the ☁️ ribbon(for mobiles，find '≡' in right-bottom corner, then click it), click it, the dashboard will be displayed like this:
![[sync-vault-dashboard.png]]
> [!note] There are four cards in dashboard: 
> 1. Vault info card.
> 2. Cloud disk info card.
> 3. Sync status card.
> 4. Quick action card.

### Navigate to syncing panel
1. Go to the 'Quick Action' card, 
2. click 'Cloud syncing panel'. 

A syncing control panel will be opened.

If current sync mode is [[restricted-mode | controlled mode]], a file explorer will be shown.

if current sync mode is [[auto-mode | auto mode]], a sync status view will be shown.

No panel will be opened in [[peer-to-peer-livesync | p2p mode]] (namely 'live sync mode').

> [!done] Congratulations, enjoy your syncing journey.🍻
